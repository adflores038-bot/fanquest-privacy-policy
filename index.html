YOUR PRIVACY, OUR PRIORITY

FanQuest respects your privacy. This policy explains what information is handled when you use our app.

1. INFORMATION WE COLLECT

FanQuest does not require users to create an account, log in, or register. The app does not ask users to provide personal information such as their name, email address, or phone number.

Game progress and related information may be stored locally on your device.

2. GAME PROGRESS AND DEVICE STORAGE

FanQuest may store game-related information locally on your device, including quiz progress, completed questions, scores, and trophies. This information helps maintain your game progress and is not intended to identify you personally.

Locally stored information may be removed when you clear the app's data or uninstall the application.

3. ADVERTISING AND THIRD-PARTY SERVICES

The current version of FanQuest does not integrate third-party advertising, analytics, or crash-reporting services.

We plan to introduce advertising in a future version. Before advertising or other third-party services are integrated, this Privacy Policy will be updated to explain the information those services may collect or process and their applicable privacy practices.

4. CHILDREN'S PRIVACY

FanQuest is not specifically directed toward children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

5. DATA SECURITY

We take reasonable steps to protect information handled by the application. Game-related information stored locally is subject to the security of your device.

6. DATA RETENTION AND DELETION

Game progress stored locally remains on your device until it is deleted by the application, cleared through your device settings, or removed when you uninstall the application.

7. CHANGES TO THIS PRIVACY POLICY

We may update this Privacy Policy when FanQuest introduces new features or services, including advertising. Any changes will be reflected in an updated policy with a revised effective date.
